# Data Logger Handling Note

## For shipping operators on the dock

Every cold chain shipment leaves with a logger, and the logger is the only witness to what happened to the product on the road. This note is a floor-level guide to handling loggers of the LGR-2210 to LGR-2260 series from issue to download. The procedure behind it is SOP-WH-088; read that first if you have not yet done so.

## Issue and start

Take a logger from the docking station only after its light shows a full battery. Write the logger number on FRM-WH-401 before you do anything else with it. The logger records a reading every 10 minutes, so a shipment of 48 hours produces about 290 readings, and a gap of more than one interval in the trace is treated as a fault.

Start the logger at least 15 minutes before you begin the pack-out. Check the start against the wall clock and against the display temperature, and do not put a logger into a shipper if either check fails. A logger that does not start goes to the supervisor; you take another one and begin again.

## Placing the logger

Put the logger in the centre of the payload. Never place it against a coolant pack, against the wall of the shipper or on top of the product where the lid can press on it. For a full pallet you need two loggers, one at the top and one in the centre of the load, and both numbers go on the form.

## The pack-out clock

From the moment you open the product carton to the moment you close the shipper you have 30 minutes. If you are going to run over, stop, return the product to the cold room and start again with fresh packs. The logger will show a pack-out that was too slow as a temperature rise at the start of the trace, and QA will ask about it.

## What the trace has to look like

A refrigerated shipment is judged against the range of 2 to 8 degrees C. The trace may spend up to 90 minutes in total outside the range, provided it never goes above 15 degrees C and never below 0 degrees C. A frozen shipment may spend up to 3 hours in total above minus 15 degrees C. If the trace is worse than that, the shipment goes into quarantine at the receiving site, and QA has to be told within 8 hours of the download.

## Transit limits

The longest journey you may plan is the validated time of the shipper less 12 hours. That means SHP-12 may travel for 60 hours and SHP-08 for 36 hours. If the carrier's forecast is longer, choose the bigger shipper, and if there is none, talk to the warehouse supervisor before you pack.

## At the receiving end

The person at the destination downloads the logger within 48 hours of receipt and sends you the trace. Do not assume that a shipment arrived well because nobody has complained: chase the trace until you have it, and file it with the shipment record.

## Common problems

- The logger was started too early and its battery ran short on a long lane.
- The logger was placed against a frozen pack and recorded a false low reading.
- The form has a logger number but the logger in the shipper is a different one.
- The logger case was cracked and the memory could not be read at the other end.
- The trace was downloaded but never sent back.
