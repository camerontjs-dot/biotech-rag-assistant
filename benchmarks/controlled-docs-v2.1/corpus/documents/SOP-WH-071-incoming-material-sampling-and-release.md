# Incoming Material Sampling and Release

## Purpose and scope

Material that arrives at the receiving dock is not yet material that can be used. This procedure describes the checks made on receipt, the way samples are taken, how much is sampled from each delivery, and the conditions under which a lot is released to production. It applies to excipients, buffer salts, glass vials, rubber stoppers and other materials delivered by approved suppliers. Approval of the suppliers themselves is covered by SOP-WH-064.

## Receipt inspection

The receiving operator inspects every delivery within 1 working day of arrival, at the materials airlock RM-330 (Grade D). The inspection checks that the supplier appears on the approved supplier list, that the delivery matches the purchase order in material, quantity and lot number, that the containers are intact, clean and correctly labelled, that seals are unbroken, and that the paperwork includes a certificate of analysis. The result is recorded on FRM-WH-510. A delivery that fails any check is not booked into stock and is reported to the warehouse supervisor.

## Quarantine

Every lot is booked into stock as quarantined and each container receives a yellow quarantine label showing the material code, the lot and the date of receipt. Quarantined stock is kept in a marked area, physically separate from released stock, and access is limited to warehouse staff. The status changes to released only when QC has released the lot and the change has been entered in the system. A quarantine label is removed only by the person who applies the release label, and the two actions are done together within 1 day of the QC release. Quarantined stock of ambient materials is held at 15 to 25 degrees C, and cold-chain materials at 2 to 8 degrees C.

## Sampling plan

The number of containers sampled depends on the number in the lot. The plan below applies to materials received in discrete containers.

| Containers in the lot | Containers sampled |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | All |
| 4 to 10 | 4 |
| 11 to 30 | 6 |
| 31 to 100 | 11 |
| More than 100 | 14 |

The sampling plan for bulk liquids delivered by tanker or in large tanks follows the sampling procedure SPL-0009. Containers are chosen at random, and the sampler records which were chosen and how, so that the choice can be checked.

## Sampling technique

Samples are taken in the sampling booth RM-334 (Grade C), which has unidirectional airflow and is cleaned at least 15 minutes before every sampling session. The sampler is gowned as for Grade C, uses clean sampling tools for each container, and reseals every container immediately after sampling with a tamper label that carries the date and initials. Powders are sampled from the top, middle and bottom of the container with a sampling thief, and the portions are combined only when the plan says so. A powder sample is at least 100 g, a liquid sample at least 500 mL, and the airflow in the booth is verified each day at 0.45 m/s. Samples reach QC within 4 hours, and the release label is applied within 2 hours of the QC release. The sampler records the number of containers, the amount taken and the booth cleaning record on FRM-WH-515.

## Identity testing

Every container of an excipient is tested for identity, not only the sampled containers, since a mix-up in a warehouse would otherwise go unseen. QC uses a rapid identity method, and the containers are marked as tested. Vials and stoppers are not identity-tested container by container; they are checked against the label and by inspection. The acceptance criteria for an excipient such as MAT-4012 come from its material specification MSP-4012.

## Inspection of primary packaging

For glass vials (MAT-4030) and rubber stoppers (MAT-4055), a visual inspection of 125 units per lot is made against the defect list of the specification, in addition to dimensional checks by QC. A defect in a critical category causes the lot to be rejected regardless of the count, and a major defect count above the acceptance number in the specification also causes rejection. Samples are inspected under the light and against the backgrounds named in the inspection instruction.

## Reliance on the certificate of analysis

A supplier's certificate of analysis may be relied upon for the tests it covers once QC has verified 3 consecutive lots from that supplier and found the certificates to be correct. The verification tests are full tests carried out by QC, and the results are compared with the certificate. Reliance is withdrawn immediately if a lot fails, if the supplier's status becomes conditional or suspended, or if the supplier changes site or process.

## Materials shipped under temperature control

For a cold-chain material, the receiving operator checks the shipping logger before the delivery is accepted and downloads the trace. A delivery whose logger shows an excursion beyond the allowance in SOP-WH-088 is rejected on receipt or placed in quarantine for a QA decision, and a deviation is raised under SOP-QA-101. A material that arrives without a working logger is treated as having failed its temperature check.

## Release

QC releases a lot within 10 working days of sampling, once all tests are complete and the results meet the specification. The release is entered into the system on FRM-WH-510 by an authorised QC signatory, and the release label is applied to each container. Where tests are pending, the lot stays in quarantine; the site does not release a lot on the strength of tests that are still running.

## Retest

Excipients are retested 24 months after manufacture and at every 12 months afterwards, and they are returned to quarantine until the retest is complete. A lot that fails a retest is rejected. Retest dates are shown on the container label and in the system, and the warehouse receives a reminder 30 days ahead, and the warehouse operator checks them at each picking.

## Rejection and return

A rejected lot is labelled, moved to the rejected area within 2 days and either returned to the supplier or destroyed. The rejection is entered in the supplier's performance record, where 2 rejected lots in 12 months change the supplier's status as described in SOP-WH-064. The supplier is notified within 2 working days of the rejection, with the test data.

## Records

Receipt, sampling and release records are kept as set in POL-0004. The paper questionnaire once used for supplier assessment under SOP-WH-019 is no longer in use, and the qualification status of each supplier is read from the approved supplier list.
