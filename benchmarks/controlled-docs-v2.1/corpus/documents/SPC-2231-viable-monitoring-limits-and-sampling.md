# Viable Monitoring Limits and Sampling Frequencies

## Purpose

This specification fixes the numerical limits, sampling frequencies and incubation conditions used to judge the microbiological and particle condition of the aseptic suites. SOP-QC-214 describes how samples are taken; this document is the single place where the numbers are set, and the procedures cite it rather than repeat it.

## Rooms and grades covered

The limits apply to the following spaces.

- ZN-A1, the Grade A zone inside barrier EQ-0602 on filling line FL-2.
- RM-301 (Grade B) and RM-304 (Grade B), the filling room and the stopper and closure preparation room, together with the final gowning room RM-291 (Grade B).
- RM-289 (Grade C), the first change room, RM-312 (Grade C) and RM-318 (Grade C), formulation and crimping.
- RM-325 (Grade D) and RM-330 (Grade D), washing, staging and the materials airlock.

Results from operators' gloves and gowns are judged under SOP-QC-219 and carry their own limits there.

## Viable air limits

Active air samples are drawn with sampler EQ-0412 or EQ-0413 at a volume of 1000 L and reported per cubic metre.

| Grade | Alert level | Action level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (ZN-A1) | not applicable | 1 CFU per m3 |
| B | 3 CFU per m3 | 7 CFU per m3 |
| C | 45 CFU per m3 | 80 CFU per m3 |
| D | 110 CFU per m3 | 200 CFU per m3 |

Any recovery from ZN-A1 is an action-level event, whichever organism grows.

## Settle plate limits

Settle plates are 90 mm plates (MAT-2231, or MAT-2236 for fungal recovery). Counts are per plate for the full permitted exposure.

| Grade | Alert level | Action level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (ZN-A1) | not applicable | 1 CFU per plate |
| B | 1 CFU per plate | 3 CFU per plate |
| C | 20 CFU per plate | 35 CFU per plate |
| D | 60 CFU per plate | 100 CFU per plate |

A plate may be exposed for a maximum of 4 hours. When a filling session is shorter than 4 hours the plate stays open for the whole session and is closed at its end.

## Contact plate limits

Contact plates are the 55 mm TSA type (MAT-2233). Where a surface has just been disinfected, the neutralizer plate MAT-2240 is used in its place.

| Grade | Alert level | Action level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (ZN-A1) | not applicable | 1 CFU per plate |
| B | 3 CFU per plate | 6 CFU per plate |
| C | 12 CFU per plate | 25 CFU per plate |
| D | 30 CFU per plate | 50 CFU per plate |

## Non-viable particle limits

Continuous counting with EQ-0417 applies to ZN-A1 during operation. The action limit for particles of 0.5 um and larger is 2900 particles per m3, and for particles of 5.0 um and larger it is 24 particles per m3. The alert level is 60 percent of the action limit in each size class.

## Sampling frequency

| Space | Frequency | Air locations | Settle positions | Contact sites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ZN-A1 | every filling session | 1 at the start of fill | 5 | 8 at session end |
| RM-301 | every filling session | 6 | 4 | 8 at session end |
| RM-304 | every filling session | 3 | 2 | 4 at session end |
| RM-291 | every filling session | 2 | 1 | 3 at session end |
| RM-289 | weekly | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| RM-312 | weekly | 4 | 2 | 5 |
| RM-318 | weekly | 4 | 2 | 5 |
| Grade D rooms | every 14 days | 3 | 2 | 4 |

## Handling and incubation

Exposed plates must reach an incubator within 5 hours of collection. TSA plates are incubated at 30 to 35 degrees C for 5 days in EQ-0421, and SDA plates at 20 to 25 degrees C for 7 days in EQ-0422. Irradiated plates are used within 42 days of manufacture.

## Trend rule

Three consecutive alert-level results at one location within 14 days start a trend investigation, even when no single result reached the action level.

## Identification requirements

Isolates from Grade A and Grade B locations are identified to species. Isolates from Grade C and Grade D locations are identified only when the action level is exceeded. Identification of a Grade A or Grade B isolate is reported within 5 working days of the request.

## Reading a result

A count that equals or exceeds the alert level is reported as an alert, and a count that equals or exceeds the action level is reported as an action. Counts on plates that were exposed for less than the permitted time are still compared with the full-exposure limits; they are never scaled up. Where a location gives no growth on a plate that was dropped, damaged or left uncovered, the plate is recorded as void on FRM-QC-117 and the location is sampled again in the same session or on the next working day.
