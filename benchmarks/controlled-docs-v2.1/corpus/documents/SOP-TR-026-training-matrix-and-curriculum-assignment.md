# Training Matrix and Curriculum Assignment

## Purpose

An employee should do only the tasks for which they have been trained and shown to be competent. This procedure explains how the site defines the training each role needs, how that training is assigned, delivered, assessed and recorded, and what happens when it is late. It applies to employees, agency staff and contractors who work in GMP areas.

## The training matrix

The training matrix is a table that links each role to the documents and skills required for it. Each row is a curriculum item, such as an SOP, a practical skill or a safety course, and each column is a role. A cell states whether the item is required, and whether it must be completed before independent work or within a set period. The matrix is owned by the training coordinator, maintained in the learning system and reviewed with department heads every 12 months and whenever a role changes materially.

## Curriculum by role

| Role | Typical curriculum |
| --- | --- |
| Line operator | GMP induction, area SOPs, gowning qualification, cleaning and line clearance, deviation reporting |
| QC analyst | GMP induction, laboratory SOPs, data integrity, methods for the assigned tests, deviation reporting |
| Warehouse operator | GMP induction, receiving and quarantine, cold room and freezer operation, shipping |
| Engineer | GMP induction, calibration programme, change control, work in classified areas |
| QA reviewer | GMP induction, deviation, CAPA and change control, batch record review, audit skills |

Roles that work in the classified areas also complete the gowning qualification programme in SOP-TR-021 before entering.

## Assigning training

A curriculum is assigned automatically when a person is entered into the learning system with a role, and it is updated when the role changes. The line manager reviews the assignment in the first week and confirms it, and adds anything specific to the person's tasks. When a document is revised under SOP-QA-105, the learning system assigns the new version to everyone whose role requires it, and the effective date of the document cannot pass until the assignment has been made.

## New starters

A new starter completes the GMP induction and the role-critical items before working independently. Until then the person works under supervision, and the supervisor is named in the matrix entry. Role-critical items are those whose omission could harm product or people, such as gowning, aseptic technique and equipment operation. Other items are completed within the period set in the matrix, usually the first 30 days.

## Read-and-understand

When a revised SOP takes effect, each person who needs it reads it and confirms that they have understood it in the learning system. The confirmation must be completed within 10 working days of the effective date. A revision that changes practice is also explained by the supervisor at a short briefing, and the briefing is recorded. A person who has questions after reading asks the document owner before confirming, not afterwards.

## Assessments

Where a course has an assessment, the pass mark is 85 percent. A person who fails has a second attempt after reviewing the material with the trainer, and the marks are entered on FRM-TR-110. A person who fails the second attempt does not attempt a third; instead the person is retrained by the trainer in a supervised session, and the retraining is recorded. Assessments are marked by the trainer and moderated by the training coordinator on a sample of 1 in 10 papers.

## Competence sign-off

Practical skills are signed off by observation. A person carries out the task 3 times under the eyes of a qualified trainer, and the trainer signs the record after the third correct execution. The executions are separate, on different occasions if possible, and a task that goes wrong resets the count. Sign-off is for a named task and a named piece of equipment, and it is repeated when the task or the equipment changes materially.

## Trainers

A trainer must hold the competence they train, have completed the trainer course, and have been observed delivering a training session. Trainer qualification is renewed every 24 months by a repeat observation and a review of the trainer's assessments. The renewal interval for contractors who train on the site's behalf follows the contractor training agreement CTA-0012, and the training coordinator keeps a list of qualified trainers by topic.

## Overdue training

Training that is not completed by its due date is reported to the line manager the next working day. When required training is overdue by more than 5 working days, the person's access to the controlled areas is suspended until it is completed, and the area supervisor is informed. A person whose access has been suspended does not carry out the tasks that the overdue training covers. Overdue counts are reported to the department heads every month and to the QA Head every quarter.

## Contractors and agency staff

Contractors and agency staff are entered on the matrix from their first day. They complete the GMP induction and the role-critical items before working on site, and their training records are held in the same system as those of employees. A contractor's employer is told which items are needed before the contractor arrives, so that access is not delayed.

## Training as a corrective action

Where a deviation or a CAPA action calls for retraining, the training is assigned through the learning system so that the completion is recorded, and the assignment refers to the record that caused it. Retraining alone is not accepted as a complete corrective action by the CAPA process in SOP-QA-172 unless the cause was shown to be lack of knowledge.

## Records

Training records show who was trained, on what, when, by whom and with what result. They are retained as set in POL-0004 and are available for audit at all times. Certificates from external courses are scanned into the record, and the original is returned to the employee.
