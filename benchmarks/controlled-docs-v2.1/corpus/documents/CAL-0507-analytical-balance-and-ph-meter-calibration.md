# Analytical Balance and pH Meter Calibration Reference

## What this note covers

This reference lists the tests, acceptance values and daily checks for the analytical balances EQ-0801 and EQ-0802, the top-loading balance EQ-0806 and the pH meters EQ-0815 and EQ-0816 in Quality Control Chemistry and Formulation. The intervals at which the full calibrations are repeated are held in SOP-EN-131, and the recording form for a full calibration is FRM-EN-201. Daily balance checks are logged on FRM-QC-141.

## Balance environment

Balances stand on a dedicated stone or damped bench in RM-410 (Grade D), away from air supply diffusers, doors and the cold surface of an outside wall. The room temperature is read at the start of each use day. A balance that has just been powered up warms for 60 minutes before any calibration or check. The pan and draught shield are cleaned with a lint-free wipe and 70 percent isopropyl alcohol at the end of each day, and any spilled material is removed at once because it changes the reading and can corrode the pan.

## Full calibration of an analytical balance

The technician uses the check-weight set EQ-0820 and works through five tests in this order.

| Test | Procedure | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensitivity | Read the 100 g weight after internal adjustment | Within plus or minus 0.5 mg |
| Low-mass check | Read the 1 g weight | Within plus or minus 0.1 mg |
| Linearity | Read five weights spread across the capacity | Each within its own certificate tolerance |
| Repeatability | Read a 50 g weight 10 times | RSD not more than 0.10 percent |
| Eccentricity | Read a 50 g weight at the four quarters of the pan | Each within plus or minus 0.5 mg of the centre reading |

The as-found reading is taken before any adjustment and the as-left reading after, and both are entered on FRM-EN-201. If the as-found reading fails, the technician stops, tags the balance and reports the finding, because an out-of-tolerance impact assessment under SOP-EN-131 is needed before the balance goes back into use.

## Daily balance check

At the first use each day, the analyst places the 100 g and the 1 g check weights on the pan in turn and records the readings on FRM-QC-141. The 100 g reading must be within plus or minus 0.3 mg of the certified value, and the 1 g reading within plus or minus 0.05 mg. A daily check is not a calibration: it is a quick test that the balance has not moved since it was last calibrated, and it uses a tighter limit for that reason. A balance that fails its daily check is tagged out, no weighing is done on it, and the Engineering supervisor is called. Readings from the previous day are reviewed by the supervisor for signs that the failure began earlier.

Any drift limit for long weighings made over several hours comes from the manufacturer's manual for the balance model.

## pH meter calibration

A pH meter is calibrated with three buffers: pH 4.01 (MAT-2601), pH 7.00 (MAT-2602) and pH 10.01 (MAT-2603), in that order, rinsing the electrode with purified water between buffers. After calibration the electrode slope must be between 95 and 105 percent, and a pH 7.00 buffer read as a sample must agree within plus or minus 0.05 pH. A slope outside the range is investigated for a worn electrode, a contaminated junction or an old buffer before the meter is adjusted again.

Buffers are discarded 30 days after the bottle is opened, and any buffer poured into a beaker is discarded 8 hours after it was decanted. During continuous use the analyst reads the pH 7.00 buffer again every 4 hours, and a drift beyond the reading tolerance stops the run until the meter has been recalibrated. Electrodes are stored in the storage solution recommended by the manufacturer and are never left to dry.

## Recording and review

Completed FRM-EN-201 forms are reviewed by the Engineering supervisor within 5 working days. A reviewer checks that the as-found and as-left readings are both present, that the certificate numbers of the check weights match the set in use, and that the label on the instrument shows the new due date. Where the calibration was carried out on a balance used for release testing, QC Chemistry counter-signs the form. Earlier paper entries in bench logbooks kept under SOP-EN-044 are not continued.

## Reference

Intervals, classes and the handling of out-of-tolerance findings follow SOP-EN-131. Retention of the forms follows POL-0004.
