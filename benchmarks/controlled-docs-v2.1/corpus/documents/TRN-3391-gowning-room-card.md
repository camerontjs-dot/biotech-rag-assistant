# Gowning Room Card

## About this card

This card hangs in the final gowning room RM-291 and is written for operators who gown for the Grade B rooms and for the Grade A zone ZN-A1. It puts the key numbers and the order of steps on one page. The controlling documents are SOP-TR-021 and SOP-QC-219. If the card ever differs from them, follow the documents and tell the qualification coordinator.

## Before you gown

Leave everything personal in RM-289: watch, rings, phone, cosmetics and any loose item. Check that you have no cough, no open wound and no skin condition that could shed. If you do, tell your supervisor before you go further. Gown packs (MAT-6010) are in the cabinet and the sterile gloves (MAT-6015) are on the shelf beside it; always take the oldest pack.

## The sequence in short

The gowning sequence has 14 steps. The important points are these.

- Sanitise your hands for 20 seconds with 70 percent isopropyl alcohol before opening the pack, and again after the gloves are on.
- Put the hood on first so that no hair shows, then the mask, then the goggles.
- Step into the coverall without letting it touch the floor, and fasten it from bottom to top.
- Tuck the hood into the collar and put the second boot covers over the trouser cuffs.
- Put on the gloves over the cuffs, sanitise them, and check yourself in the mirror.

## How long your qualification lasts

You are qualified for a grade after 2 successful sessions on separate days. If you work in Grade B you requalify every 12 months, and if you work only in Grade C or D you requalify every 24 months. Your next due date is on the register that hangs beside the door. If you have not entered Grade B for 90 days, your qualification lapses and you repeat a session before you work.

## The numbers that decide a session

- Glove prints must show no growth.
- Each gown site must show no more than 5 colonies per plate.
- If a session fails, you have at least 2 hours of retraining before you try again.
- Two failures within 30 days mean a 14-day stand-down from Grade B.

## Gloves and garments

Outer gloves for work in ZN-A1 are changed after 3 hours of continuous wear, and at once if they are damaged or touch anything that is not sterile. Sterile gowns are retired after 25 cycles, so look at the cycle count on the label and put back any gown that is close to the limit.

## If you are unwell

Tell your supervisor. After an illness with fever, cough or diarrhoea, do not enter Grade B until 72 hours after your symptoms have gone.

## Do not

- Do not enter RM-291 with wet hands or wet hair.
- Do not touch the outside of the gown after it is on, except for the buttons you have to fasten.
- Do not hold or prop the interlocked doors.
- Do not carry pens, paper or personal items into the clean side.
- Do not walk fast in the gowning room or in the clean areas.
