# Operator Card: Cleaning Steps and Holds

## About this card

This card is kept in the washing room RM-325 and is written for line operators who clean product-contact parts and surfaces on filling line FL-2. It gives the order of the steps and the numbers you need at the sink. The full rules are in SOP-MF-207 and the values behind them are in SPC-2260. If the card and those documents disagree, follow the documents and tell your supervisor.

## The clocks

Two clocks run on every part, and both are on the tag.

- Dirty hold: you have 36 hours from the end of processing to start cleaning. If you cannot start in time, tell your supervisor before the limit, not after.
- Clean hold: washed parts stay good for 96 hours when stored covered on the clean side of RM-325, and wrapped sterilised parts for 10 days.

Write the time on the tag every time the part changes state. A part with no time on its tag is treated as past its hold.

## The agents

- Alkaline detergent MAT-3101 is used at 3.0 percent. Leave it in contact for 15 minutes at 55 degrees C.
- Acid descaler MAT-3105 is used once a month at 2.0 percent.
- Sporicidal disinfectant MAT-3110 is ready to use and stays wet on the surface for 15 minutes.
- Isopropyl alcohol MAT-3112 at 70 percent is for gloves and tools, 30 seconds of contact.

Prepared solutions are good for 14 days from the day you make them, and opened concentrate for 28 days. Write the date you prepared or opened on the container before you put the lid on.

## The steps

1. Rinse the part with purified water straight after the run.
2. Wash it with the alkaline detergent at the strength and temperature above.
3. Rinse it twice with WFI.
4. Check the final rinse conductivity. It must read 5.0 uS/cm or lower.
5. Use the acid descaler in the month it is due, and rinse again.
6. Dry the part, inspect it under the bench light and cover it.
7. Fill in the tag and FRM-MF-301 before you walk away.

## Surface disinfection

Use the sporicidal agent on Grade B surfaces every 2 weeks and on Grade C surfaces once a month. Wipe from the cleanest point to the dirtiest, from high to low, and use a fresh wipe for each area. Never dry-wipe the barrier.

## Do not

- Do not top up an old bottle of solution with fresh solution.
- Do not use an agent that is not on this card.
- Do not stack wet parts on each other.
- Do not put a part on the floor, even for a moment.
- Do not sign the tag for a step you did not see done.

## When something goes wrong

If a hold has passed, the part goes back to the start of the cleaning sequence. If the rinse conductivity is over the limit, rinse again and read again, and if it is still high, call your supervisor. If you find residue after the part has been signed off, stop, keep the part where it is and report it the same shift.
