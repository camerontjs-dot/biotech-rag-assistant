# Supplier Questionnaire Practice

## Purpose

New suppliers are assessed on paper before a first order is placed. This practice describes the printed questionnaire that the warehouse posts to a supplier, how the answers are scored, and how the scored questionnaire is filed. It applies to suppliers of materials and packaging.

## The questionnaire

The questionnaire has 90 questions in 9 sections: quality system, personnel, premises, equipment, materials, production, laboratory, complaints and recalls, and documentation. Each question is answered yes, no or not applicable, with a line for a comment. The warehouse buyer posts the pack to the supplier's quality contact with a covering letter, and enters the date of posting in the supplier register.

## Return of the questionnaire

The supplier is asked to return the completed questionnaire within 45 days. A reminder is posted after 30 days if nothing has been received. A questionnaire that comes back with more than 10 unanswered questions is returned to the supplier for completion, and the return date is recorded again.

## Scoring

The buyer scores each section by counting the number of yes answers as a proportion of the applicable questions. A supplier scores acceptably if every section reaches 80 percent and the total reaches 85 percent. A section below 80 percent is discussed with the QA reviewer, who decides whether to ask for an explanation, to plan a visit or to decline the supplier. The scoring sheet is signed by the buyer and the QA reviewer.

## Reminders and escalation

A supplier that has not answered after 30 days receives a reminder by post, and a second reminder follows at 60 days with a copy to the supplier's commercial contact. After 90 days the nomination is cancelled, the questionnaire is marked as not returned in the register, and the buyer is told that no order may be placed with the supplier. A supplier may be nominated again after 6 months, and the whole process starts again with a fresh questionnaire.

## Confidentiality

The answers in the questionnaire are commercially sensitive. The binder is kept in a locked cabinet in the supply office, and copies are made only for the QA reviewer. Supplier information is never shared with another supplier, and staff who visit a supplier do not disclose what other suppliers have said.

## Filing

The original questionnaire, the scoring sheet and the covering letter are kept in a binder in the supply office, arranged alphabetically by supplier name. A copy of the scoring sheet goes to QA. The binder is reviewed every year by the warehouse supervisor, who checks that every supplier on the register has a scored questionnaire that is less than 5 years old.

## Limitations

The paper questionnaire records what the supplier says about itself, and the practice provides no way to check the answers except by a visit. Suppliers that score below the threshold are visited before approval, and suppliers that score above it are approved without a visit unless their material is used directly in product.
