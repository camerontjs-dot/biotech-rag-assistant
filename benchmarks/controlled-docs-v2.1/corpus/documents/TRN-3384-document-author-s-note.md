# Document Author's Note

## For people who write or revise controlled documents

This note is a working summary for authors and document owners. It shows the path a document takes from draft to effective version and the dates that authors are held to. The controlling procedure is SOP-QA-105, and retention periods are in POL-0004. If this note and the procedure differ, follow the procedure and tell the document controller.

## Before you start

Do not begin a revision by editing the effective file. Ask the document controller for a draft copy, and start the request on FRM-QA-501 with the reason for the change, the scope and the effect on training. Document numbers come only from the document controller; you may not choose your own, and you may not reuse the number of a withdrawn document.

## The path of a document

1. You write the draft from the copy issued to you.
2. The document controller circulates it to the reviewers.
3. Reviewers reply within 15 working days. A reviewer with no comments must say so.
4. You answer every comment, and unresolved comments go to the owner and QA.
5. QA approves within 10 working days of the last comment closing.
6. The effective date is at least 10 working days after approval, and never before the affected staff have finished read-and-understand training.
7. On the effective date the new version replaces the old one everywhere and the old one goes to the archive folder.

## Version numbers

If the change alters what people do, the version goes up by a whole number, for example 3.0 to 4.0. If it only corrects a typing error or a cross-reference, the version goes up by 0.1 and no training is needed.

## Reviews

Every SOP is reviewed every 36 months, every specification every 48 months, and every training note every 24 months. Policies are reviewed every 36 months. The document controller reminds you 15 days before a review is due, so put the date in your calendar too. On FRM-QA-505 you record one of four results: no change, minor revision, major revision or withdrawal.

## Printed copies

A printed copy is valid for 48 hours after the print stamp on its face. Do not photocopy a controlled document, and do not keep a copy at your desk beyond that time. If you need a copy at the bench, ask for it on FRM-QA-510.

## Drafts

A draft is not to be used to carry out any activity, however close it is to approval. If someone at the bench asks you which version to follow, the answer is always the effective one, even if you know the draft is better.

## Common problems

- Editing the effective file to fix a small mistake.
- Sending a draft to one reviewer and assuming the others agree.
- Setting an effective date before training is done.
- Forgetting to update the cross-references in other documents after a renumbering.
- Leaving a review to the day it falls due.
