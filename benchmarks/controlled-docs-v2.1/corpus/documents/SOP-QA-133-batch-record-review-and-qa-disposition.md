# Batch Record Review and QA Disposition

## Purpose

A batch is released on the strength of its record. This procedure describes how the executed batch record and its supporting data are reviewed at three levels, production, QC and QA, how errors and gaps in the record are handled, and how QA reaches and documents the disposition of the batch. It applies to every drug product batch made at the site. The policy that sets out who may release and on what grounds is POL-0012.

## The three reviews

| Stage | Reviewer | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-line review | Production supervisor not involved in the batch | 2 working days from batch completion |
| QC data review | QC reviewer | 3 working days from the last test result |
| QA review | QA batch reviewer | 5 working days from receipt of the complete package |

The target for the whole cycle, from the completion of the batch to the disposition decision, is 15 working days. The clock runs on working days, and it pauses only while a query is with the originating department. The QA batch reviewer records the pause on the checklist so that the delay is visible. A query is answered by the originating department within 2 days, the QA batch reviewer restarts the clock on the day the answer arrives, and a query that stays open for more than 5 days is escalated to the head of that department. The QA Head reviews the list of batches waiting for disposition every 7 days.

## First-line review

The production reviewer checks that every page of the record is present and numbered, that every step is signed and timed, that critical process parameters were within their ranges, that the equipment used is identified and was clean and in calibration, and that materials are traceable to received lots. Any entry outside a range, any missing signature and any correction that is not properly made is listed as a query on FRM-QA-401 and returned to the operator. The reviewer signs the first-line section only when no query is open.

## QC data review

The QC reviewer confirms that every test named in the specification was done on the right sample, that results are within limits, that the raw data are attached or referenced, and that any out-of-specification or invalid result has been through the investigation route of the OOS procedure OSP-0006. Stability commitments and the release of starting materials used in the batch are also checked. QC reports the package as complete on FRM-QA-401.

## QA review

The QA batch reviewer works through the complete package in the order of the checklist FRM-QA-401: the record, the first-line and QC sign-offs, reconciliations, deviations, environmental monitoring, and the supporting documents. The reviewer does not simply re-check the earlier reviews; the QA review looks for what the earlier reviews cannot see, such as a pattern across steps, a parameter that is inside its range but at its edge, or an entry that conflicts with another record.

## Documentation practices

Corrections are made with a single line through the entry, with the initials of the person making the correction, the date and the reason. Correction fluid must not be used, and an entry that has been overwritten or obscured is treated as a data integrity finding. A late entry, made after the event because a step was not recorded at the time, is allowed only if it states the reason and is countersigned by the supervisor within 1 working day. An empty field is never left blank: the operator writes not applicable or draws a line and initials it. A signature stamp or a shared login is not accepted.

## Reconciliation

Yield is reconciled against the theoretical yield, and the result must be within plus or minus 2.0 percent. Anything outside that range is investigated as a deviation before the batch can proceed to QA review. Label reconciliation is done separately; the variance must be zero, with used, returned and destroyed labels adding up to the number issued. Reconciliation of components and packaging materials follows the same principle, with a variance of no more than 0.5 percent for stoppers and seals, and the numbers are written in the record and initialled by two people. The QA batch reviewer also verifies at least 10 percent of the critical entries against the source data, such as balance printouts and equipment logs. Yield limits for a new product come from the master batch record MBR-0210 for that product until the site has enough batches to set its own.

## Environmental monitoring and utilities

The QA batch reviewer reviews the environmental monitoring results for the filling session or sessions of the batch, as reported under SOP-QC-214. Any action-level result in Grade A or Grade B during the session has to have been through a deviation and been closed before the batch is dispositioned. Alert-level results are considered for their trend, and the reviewer notes whether the same location has been at alert level in 2 or more sessions of the batch.

## Deviations and holds

A deviation on the batch, whatever its class, means the batch is on hold until the deviation is closed or an exception is granted. The reviewer confirms that each deviation reference in the record matches the deviation register and that the batch impact assessment has been done under SOP-QA-101.

## Exceptions

The QA Head may grant an exception that allows disposition while a deviation is still open, on FRM-QA-408. An exception is valid for one batch only. It must state why waiting would harm patients or supply, what the open risk is and what will be done to close it. An exception is never granted where the deviation is Critical.

## Disposition

The QA batch reviewer recommends a disposition, and the release authority named in POL-0012 makes the decision. The decision is recorded on the disposition certificate FRM-QA-405 within 1 working day, a copy of the certificate reaches the warehouse within 1 day so that stock can move out of quarantine, and the record is closed and archived within 30 days of disposition. The categories are Released, Rejected and Held; conditional release is not permitted. A batch that cannot be released within its cycle target is reported to the QA Head with the reason and an estimated date.

## Retain samples

For each released lot, 20 units are placed in the retain store, labelled with the lot, the date and the storage condition, held at the labelled temperature, and their retention period follows POL-0012. The reviewer confirms that the retain sample has been taken and recorded before signing the disposition.

## Metrics

QA reviewers handle no more than 8 batch records in the same week, so that each gets a full review. QA reports by the 5th working day of every month the average number of working days from batch completion to disposition, the number of queries per batch, and the proportion of batches with a late entry. A rise in queries for one product or one area is reviewed with the department head. Records that come back with more than 10 queries are returned to the department for a rewrite, and the count is discussed at the weekly quality meeting.

## Pace and cross-checks

Reviewers work in blocks of no more than 2 hours, because accuracy falls when the same person reads pages for longer. About 20 percent of the pages of every record are read a second time by another reviewer, and the environmental monitoring data for the 3 weeks before the filling session are checked as well as those of the session itself. A record that goes missing is reported to the QA Head within 4 hours of discovery. Yield and label reconciliation are checked within 15 minutes of the end of packaging, and a certificate that is more than 45 days old at the time of dispatch is confirmed again by QA. The signature specimen list is refreshed every 3 months.
