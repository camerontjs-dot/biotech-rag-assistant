# Stability Storage Conditions and Pull Schedule

## Purpose

This specification fixes the storage conditions, pull points, pull windows and sample quantities for the stability studies run in the chambers CH-01 to CH-05 and the stability store RM-415 (Grade D). SOP-QC-236 describes how studies are set up and run. The tests done at each time point are set in the stability protocol for the product, for example STP-0311 for PRD-311.

## Storage conditions

| Chamber | Study condition | Setpoint | Tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CH-01 | Long-term | 25 degrees C and 60 percent RH | Plus or minus 3 degrees C and plus or minus 5 percent RH |
| CH-02 | Intermediate | 30 degrees C and 65 percent RH | Plus or minus 3 degrees C and plus or minus 5 percent RH |
| CH-03 | Accelerated | 40 degrees C and 75 percent RH | Plus or minus 3 degrees C and plus or minus 5 percent RH |
| CH-04 | Refrigerated | 5 degrees C | Plus or minus 3 degrees C |
| CH-05 | Frozen | Minus 20 degrees C | Plus or minus 5 degrees C |

Photostability studies are run in the cabinet PC-1, and the exposure is set by the photostability study PHS-0008.

## Pull points

The months below are counted from the date the samples were placed in the chamber, which is the time zero point.

| Chamber | Pull points in months |
| --- | --- |
| CH-01 long-term | 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 |
| CH-02 intermediate | 0, 6, 9, 12 |
| CH-03 accelerated | 0, 3, 6 |
| CH-04 refrigerated | 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 |
| CH-05 frozen | 0, 6, 12, 24, 36 |

The intermediate condition is run only when a significant change occurs at the accelerated condition, and it starts as soon as that change is confirmed.

## Pull windows

A pull is valid when the samples leave the chamber within the window around the nominal date.

| Pull point | Window |
| --- | --- |
| Up to and including 6 months | Plus or minus 14 days |
| 9 and 12 months | Plus or minus 21 days |
| 18 months and later | Plus or minus 30 days |

A pull outside its window is recorded as a deviation under SOP-QA-101, and the results are reported with the actual age of the samples. The window is not extended for weekends or holidays; the pull is scheduled early instead.

## Samples per pull

Each pull removes 10 units for a product presented in vials: 8 for testing and 2 held in reserve. The reserve units stay in the chamber until the results are accepted. Where the protocol calls for extra units for a special test, such as an in-use study, the extra units are placed at time zero and are listed in the protocol, not added later.

## Batches

Two production batches of each product are placed on stability each year, and they are chosen from the batches made in the first and the second half of the year unless a change under SOP-QA-118 requires an earlier batch. Additional batches are placed after a process change that could affect stability, and after a change of container or closure.

## Reporting

Results for each time point are reported against the shelf life specification of the product, and results within 5 percent of a limit are marked for the stability coordinator. The summary report for each study year follows the timing in SOP-QC-236. The protocol may extend a study beyond the pull points above, but never shorten it without a change record.
