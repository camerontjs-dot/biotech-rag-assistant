# Batch Record Review and QA Disposition control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls batch record review and qa disposition at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing batch record review and qa disposition work in R-batch-203 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 183 CFU per unit. Action limit means 185 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 124 hours. Clean hold time means 62 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-batch-121 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-batch-407 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-batch-393 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-batch-121 status in R-batch-203 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-batch-407 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-batch-393.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 185 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 183 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition EQ-batch-121 R-batch-203 FRM-batch-393.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 62 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 124 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition EQ-batch-121 R-batch-203 FRM-batch-393.

5. Store samples at 11 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition R-batch-203 EQ-batch-121.

6. Complete review within 5 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition FRM-batch-393 R-batch-203.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition EQ-batch-121 R-batch-203.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Batch Record Review and QA Disposition R-batch-203.
