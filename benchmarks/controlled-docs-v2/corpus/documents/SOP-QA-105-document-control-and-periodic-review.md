# Document Control and Periodic Review

## Purpose

People act on documents, so a document that has been overtaken by a newer version, or that is unapproved or uncontrolled, is a hazard. This procedure governs how controlled documents are numbered, written, reviewed, approved, issued, revised, kept under periodic review and finally withdrawn. It applies to SOPs, specifications, policies, technical notes, calibration notes, training notes and worked examples used at the site. The retention periods that apply to records and superseded documents are set in POL-0004.

## Document types and numbers

Each controlled document has an identifier made of a type prefix and a number.

| Prefix | Type | Example form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SOP | Standard operating procedure | SOP-QA-105 |
| SPC | Specification | SPC-2231 |
| POL | Policy | POL-0004 |
| TCN | Technical note | TCN-4102 |
| CAL | Calibration note | CAL-0507 |
| TRN | Training note | TRN-3308 |
| DVX | Worked deviation example | DVX-5006 |

Numbers are issued only by the document controller, who keeps the register and answers a request for a number within 1 day. The register is checked against the master files every 6 months. Nobody self-assigns a number, and a number is never reused, even when the document it belonged to has been withdrawn.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Document owner | Holds the technical content, keeps it current, answers review questions |
| Author | Writes or revises the text on behalf of the owner |
| Reviewer | Checks accuracy and practicality in their own area |
| QA approver | Confirms compliance and gives final approval |
| Document controller | Issues numbers, controls the master file, distributes and archives |

## Writing and revising

A new document or a revision starts on FRM-QA-501, the change to document request, which states the reason, the scope and the training impact. A revision of a document that changes practice is also assessed under SOP-QA-118 where the change affects a validated process. The author works on a draft copy issued by the document controller, and edits to an effective file are not permitted: the effective version stays unchanged until the new version replaces it. A draft is marked as such, and a draft must never be used to carry out an activity. A draft with no activity for 90 days is returned to the owner, who confirms whether it is still needed.

## Review circulation

The document controller circulates the draft to the named reviewers, who respond within 10 working days. A reviewer who has no comments says so in writing; silence is not accepted as agreement. The author answers every comment, and an unresolved comment goes to the document owner and the QA approver. The review is complete when all comments are closed or agreed to be declined with a reason.

## Approval

The QA approver signs within 5 working days of the last review comment closing. The document owner signs before the QA approver. Approval is by electronic signature in the document system, which records the name, the meaning of the signature and the time. A document signed on paper is scanned into the system on the same day.

## Effective dates and training

The effective date is set when the document is approved. It is at least 5 working days after approval, and never before the affected staff have completed read-and-understand training under SOP-TR-026. The document controller confirms the training record before the effective date. On the effective date, the new version replaces the old one in every location and the old version moves to the archive folder as a superseded version.

## Version numbers

A change to content that alters practice raises the version by a whole number, for example from 3.0 to 4.0. A correction that does not alter practice, such as an updated cross-reference or a corrected typing error, raises the version by 0.1 and needs no read-and-understand training. The version and the effective date appear on every page of the document.

## Periodic review

Every document is reviewed for continued suitability at the interval for its type.

| Type | Review interval |
| --- | --- |
| SOP | 24 months |
| Specification | 36 months |
| Policy | 36 months |
| Training note | 12 months |

The document controller reminds the owner 30 days before the review is due. The owner records the result on FRM-QA-505 as no change, minor revision, major revision or withdrawal. A review that finds no need for change extends the review date by one interval and does not change the version number. An owner who leaves the company hands the documents to a successor in writing, and the register is updated within 5 working days.

## Overdue reviews

A document that passes its review date stays effective for up to 30 days while the review is completed, and is then flagged in the register. A document that is still overdue after 90 days is reported to the QA Head, who decides whether it may stay in use, and a decision to keep it in use is written on FRM-QA-505 with the reasons.

## Controlled copies

A printed copy of a document is valid for 24 hours after the print stamp on its face, and after that it is uncontrolled and must be destroyed or replaced. A copy is requested on FRM-QA-510 and issued by the document controller. Copies used in production areas are collected at the end of the shift where the practice allows it, so that no old copy remains at the bench. Photocopying or scanning a controlled document is not permitted.

## Superseded and obsolete documents

Superseded versions stay in the archive folder, marked with their status, so that a record made under an earlier version can still be understood. A document that is no longer needed at all is withdrawn as obsolete, and the reason is recorded. Withdrawn documents are not deleted; they are kept for the period set in POL-0004.

## Records and audit

The register, the request forms, the review records and the signature records are retained as set in POL-0004. The document controller reports overdue reviews and open drafts to QA every month, approved documents reach the shared drive within 2 hours of their effective date, read-and-understand reminders go out 3 days before they are due, the number of a withdrawn document is reserved for 20 years, and the QA Head reviews the state of the system once a year. Findings from internal audits that concern documents are managed under SOP-QA-172.
