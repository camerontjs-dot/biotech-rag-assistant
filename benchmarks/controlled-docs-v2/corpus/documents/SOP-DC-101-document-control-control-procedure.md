# Document Control control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls document control at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing document control work in R-document-226 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 81 CFU per unit. Action limit means 83 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 62 hours. Clean hold time means 31 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-document-185 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-document-461 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-document-378 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-document-185 status in R-document-226 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 8 samples using MAT-document-461 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-document-378.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 83 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 81 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Document Control EQ-document-185 R-document-226 FRM-document-378.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 31 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 62 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Document Control EQ-document-185 R-document-226 FRM-document-378.

5. Store samples at 15 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Document Control R-document-226 EQ-document-185.

6. Complete review within 10 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Document Control FRM-document-378 R-document-226.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Document Control EQ-document-185 R-document-226.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Document Control R-document-226.
