# Environmental Monitoring control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls environmental monitoring at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing environmental monitoring work in R-environmental-216 (Grade C). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 9 CFU per unit. Action limit means 11 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 14 hours. Clean hold time means 7 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-environmental-155 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-environmental-494 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-environmental-303 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-environmental-155 status in R-environmental-216 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 10 samples using MAT-environmental-494 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-environmental-303.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 11 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 9 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Environmental Monitoring EQ-environmental-155 R-environmental-216 FRM-environmental-303.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 7 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 14 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Environmental Monitoring EQ-environmental-155 R-environmental-216 FRM-environmental-303.

5. Store samples at 2 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Environmental Monitoring R-environmental-216 EQ-environmental-155.

6. Complete review within 7 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Environmental Monitoring FRM-environmental-303 R-environmental-216.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Environmental Monitoring EQ-environmental-155 R-environmental-216.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Environmental Monitoring R-environmental-216.
