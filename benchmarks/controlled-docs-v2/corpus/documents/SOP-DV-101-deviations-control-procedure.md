# Deviations control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls deviations at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing deviations work in R-deviations-271 (Grade B). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 25 CFU per unit. Action limit means 27 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 24 hours. Clean hold time means 12 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-deviations-193 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-deviations-495 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-deviations-335 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-deviations-193 status in R-deviations-271 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-deviations-495 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-deviations-335.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 27 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 25 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Deviations EQ-deviations-193 R-deviations-271 FRM-deviations-335.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 12 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 24 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Deviations EQ-deviations-193 R-deviations-271 FRM-deviations-335.

5. Store samples at 5 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Deviations R-deviations-271 EQ-deviations-193.

6. Complete review within 14 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Deviations FRM-deviations-335 R-deviations-271.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Deviations EQ-deviations-193 R-deviations-271.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Deviations R-deviations-271.
