# Water Systems control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls water systems at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to Engineering personnel performing water systems work in R-water-218 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 145 CFU per unit. Action limit means 147 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 96 hours. Clean hold time means 48 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

| Role | Responsibility |

| QA reviewer | Verify limits and sign disposition |
| Trainer | Qualify personnel before unsupervised work |

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-water-114 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-water-416 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-water-398 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-water-114 status in R-water-218 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 8 samples using MAT-water-416 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-water-398.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 147 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 145 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Water Systems EQ-water-114 R-water-218 FRM-water-398.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 48 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 96 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Water Systems EQ-water-114 R-water-218 FRM-water-398.

5. Store samples at 6 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Water Systems R-water-218 EQ-water-114.

6. Complete review within 10 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Water Systems FRM-water-398 R-water-218.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Water Systems EQ-water-114 R-water-218.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Water Systems R-water-218.
