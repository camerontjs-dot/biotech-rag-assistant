# Equipment Calibration control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls equipment calibration at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to Engineering personnel performing equipment calibration work in R-equipment-216 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 123 CFU per unit. Action limit means 125 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 88 hours. Clean hold time means 44 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-equipment-182 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-equipment-450 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-equipment-376 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-equipment-182 status in R-equipment-216 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-equipment-450 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-equipment-376.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 125 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 123 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Equipment Calibration EQ-equipment-182 R-equipment-216 FRM-equipment-376.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 44 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 88 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Equipment Calibration EQ-equipment-182 R-equipment-216 FRM-equipment-376.

5. Store samples at 3 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Equipment Calibration R-equipment-216 EQ-equipment-182.

6. Complete review within 7 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Equipment Calibration FRM-equipment-376 R-equipment-216.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Equipment Calibration EQ-equipment-182 R-equipment-216.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Equipment Calibration R-equipment-216.
