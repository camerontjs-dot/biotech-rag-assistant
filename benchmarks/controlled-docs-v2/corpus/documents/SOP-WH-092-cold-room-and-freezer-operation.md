# Cold Room and Freezer Operation

## Purpose and coverage

The warehouse holds refrigerated stock in the cold room CR-1 (room RM-140, Grade D) and frozen stock in the freezer FZ-1. A standby cold room, CR-2, takes stock from CR-1 when it cannot hold its range. This procedure sets out how these units are loaded, monitored, alarmed and recovered. Shipping from the dock is covered by SOP-WH-088.

## Operating ranges and monitoring

### Cold room CR-1

CR-1 operates between 2 and 8 degrees C with a setpoint of 5 degrees C. Two probes serve the room: EQ-0910 controls the refrigeration and EQ-0911 is an independent monitoring probe wired to the alarm panel, so that a fault in the controller cannot hide a temperature rise. Both are logged every 15 minutes on the monitoring system, and the traces are reviewed by the warehouse supervisor each working day.

A high or low temperature alarm sounds only after the reading has been outside the range for 20 minutes, so that a normal door opening or defrost cycle does not trigger it. A separate door-open alarm sounds after 3 minutes with the door ajar. The door should not be held open for more than 2 minutes for any single movement of stock, and a pallet jack is never parked in the doorway.

### Freezer FZ-1

FZ-1 operates between minus 25 and minus 15 degrees C with a setpoint of minus 20 degrees C. Its probe EQ-0915 is logged every 15 minutes, and the high temperature alarm sounds after 25 minutes outside the range. The freezer door is opened only for a full pick or a full put-away, and the operator never leaves it open while searching for a product.

## Loading and stock handling

Shelves are loaded to no more than 85 percent of capacity so that air can circulate between the pallets. Pallets and cartons are kept at least 15 cm from the walls and clear of the evaporator, and nothing is stacked in front of the return air grille. Stock is picked first-expired-first-out and each location is scanned at put-away and at picking, so that the system always shows what is inside the room. A monthly stock reconciliation compares the system with a physical count of CR-1 and FZ-1, and every difference is explained in writing before the count is signed.

Product in quarantine is kept on the marked shelves at the left of the room and is never mixed with released stock. Products that must be kept away from light are stored in their outer cartons, and cartons with damaged corners are repacked before they are put away.

## Responding to an alarm

The person on duty acknowledges a temperature alarm within 10 minutes and enters the time and the reading on FRM-WH-405. The first action is to check the door, the fans and the readings of both probes to find out whether the alarm is a real rise or a sensor fault.

1. If the door is ajar, close it and watch the trace for 10 minutes.
2. If the reading is still rising, call the on-call technician and the warehouse supervisor.
3. Note the start time of the excursion from the trace, not from the time the alarm was answered.
4. If the excursion lasts more than 30 minutes, move the stock in CR-1 to CR-2 within 2 hours, with the transfer logged on FRM-WH-405.
5. Tell QA so that a deviation is opened under SOP-QA-101.

The stock is not released from CR-2 back into general use until QA has reviewed the trace and decided its disposition.

## Power failure

If mains power to CR-1 fails, the standby generator starts within 15 seconds and takes over the refrigeration and the monitoring panel. Anyone who sees the room lights out or the panel dark checks that the generator has started, and reports it if it has not. Facilities run the generator monthly under their own maintenance programme.

## Mapping and requalification

CR-1 is mapped every 24 months and after any relocation, refit or replacement of the refrigeration plant. The mapping places probes at the corners, the centre and the door, in the empty room and in the loaded room, and the results decide where the monitoring probe stays and where product may not be placed. The interval at which the door seals of CR-1 are replaced is set by the facilities maintenance plan FMP-0003. The frequency of evaporator defrost follows the equipment manual and the controller's own programme.

## Calibration

Probes EQ-0910, EQ-0911 and EQ-0915 are calibrated as cold room and chamber probes under SOP-EN-131. A probe that is due or past due is replaced with a calibrated probe rather than left in service. After any probe change the traces of the two probes are compared for a day to confirm that they agree.

## Cleaning and inspection

CR-1 is cleaned once a month with the stock moved to CR-2 in an ordered way, and the floor is dried before stock is returned. The technician inspects door gaskets, the drain and the shelf condition each month and writes findings on the inspection sheet. Evaporator ice build-up seen at any time is reported to Facilities the same day.

## Access

Only trained warehouse operators may enter CR-1 or FZ-1. Visitors are escorted, and contractors working on the units sign in and out on the log and are told which shelves hold quarantined stock. Operators who enter the freezer wear insulated gloves and are never left alone in it.
