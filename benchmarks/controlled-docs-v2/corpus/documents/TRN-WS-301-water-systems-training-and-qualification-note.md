# Water Systems training and qualification note

## Training scope

Personnel must complete this synthetic training before unsupervised water-systems work at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01.

## Key values taught

Trainees memorize the action limit of 151 CFU per unit and the clean hold time of 51 hours for R-water-218.

Note: this training note predates the current SOP revision and states the prior values for familiarization only. The governing SOP controls current work.

## Qualification

Qualification requires observed practice and a documented attempt. Retraining is required after a failed observation or a procedural revision that changes steps.
