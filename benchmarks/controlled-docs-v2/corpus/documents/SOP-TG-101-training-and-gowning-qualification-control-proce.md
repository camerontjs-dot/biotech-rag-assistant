# Training and Gowning Qualification control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls training and gowning qualification at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to Training personnel performing training and gowning qualification work in R-training-286 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 103 CFU per unit. Action limit means 105 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 76 hours. Clean hold time means 38 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-training-197 status in R-training-286 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-training-480 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-training-332.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 105 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 103 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification EQ-training-197 R-training-286 FRM-training-332.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 38 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 76 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification EQ-training-197 R-training-286 FRM-training-332.

5. Store samples at 18 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification R-training-286 EQ-training-197.

6. Complete review within 14 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification FRM-training-332 R-training-286.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification EQ-training-197 R-training-286.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Training and Gowning Qualification R-training-286.
