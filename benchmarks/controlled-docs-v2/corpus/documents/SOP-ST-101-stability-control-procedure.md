# Stability control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls stability at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QC personnel performing stability work in R-stability-276 (Grade B). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 231 CFU per unit. Action limit means 233 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 146 hours. Clean hold time means 73 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-stability-157 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-stability-422 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-stability-353 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-stability-157 status in R-stability-276 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 8 samples using MAT-stability-422 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-stability-353.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 233 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 231 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Stability EQ-stability-157 R-stability-276 FRM-stability-353.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 73 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 146 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Stability EQ-stability-157 R-stability-276 FRM-stability-353.

5. Store samples at 18 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Stability R-stability-276 EQ-stability-157.

6. Complete review within 5 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Stability FRM-stability-353 R-stability-276.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Stability EQ-stability-157 R-stability-276.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Stability R-stability-276.
