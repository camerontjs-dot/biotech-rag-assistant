# Change Control control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls change control at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing change control work in R-change-288 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 69 CFU per unit. Action limit means 71 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 48 hours. Clean hold time means 24 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |

| QA reviewer | Verify limits and sign disposition |
| Trainer | Qualify personnel before unsupervised work |

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-change-122 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-change-485 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-change-327 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-change-122 status in R-change-288 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 10 samples using MAT-change-485 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-change-327.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 71 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 69 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Change Control EQ-change-122 R-change-288 FRM-change-327.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 24 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 48 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Change Control EQ-change-122 R-change-288 FRM-change-327.

5. Store samples at 11 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Change Control R-change-288 EQ-change-122.

6. Complete review within 7 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Change Control FRM-change-327 R-change-288.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Change Control EQ-change-122 R-change-288.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Change Control R-change-288.
