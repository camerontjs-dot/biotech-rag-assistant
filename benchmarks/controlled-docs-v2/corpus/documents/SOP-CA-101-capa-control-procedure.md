# CAPA control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls capa at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing capa work in R-capa-212 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 49 CFU per unit. Action limit means 51 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 40 hours. Clean hold time means 20 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-capa-129 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-capa-488 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-capa-391 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-capa-129 status in R-capa-212 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 5 samples using MAT-capa-488 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-capa-391.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 51 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 49 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: CAPA EQ-capa-129 R-capa-212 FRM-capa-391.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 20 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 40 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: CAPA EQ-capa-129 R-capa-212 FRM-capa-391.

5. Store samples at 8 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: CAPA R-capa-212 EQ-capa-129.

6. Complete review within 10 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: CAPA FRM-capa-391 R-capa-212.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: CAPA EQ-capa-129 R-capa-212.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: CAPA R-capa-212.
