# Complaints and Returns control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls complaints and returns at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QA personnel performing complaints and returns work in R-complaints-203 (Grade C). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 265 CFU per unit. Action limit means 267 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 172 hours. Clean hold time means 86 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-complaints-179 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-complaints-424 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-complaints-332 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-complaints-179 status in R-complaints-203 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-complaints-424 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-complaints-332.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 267 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 265 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Complaints and Returns EQ-complaints-179 R-complaints-203 FRM-complaints-332.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 86 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 172 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Complaints and Returns EQ-complaints-179 R-complaints-203 FRM-complaints-332.

5. Store samples at 5 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Complaints and Returns R-complaints-203 EQ-complaints-179.

6. Complete review within 7 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Complaints and Returns FRM-complaints-332 R-complaints-203.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Complaints and Returns EQ-complaints-179 R-complaints-203.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Complaints and Returns R-complaints-203.
