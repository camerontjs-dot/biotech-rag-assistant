# Water Quality Specification: PW and WFI

## Scope

This specification sets the chemical and microbiological alert and action levels for purified water (PW) from loop LP-01 and for water for injection (WFI) from loop LP-02. SOP-EN-152 describes how and how often the two loops are sampled, and SOP-EN-158 describes their sanitization. The levels apply at the sample points PT-01 to PT-12 on the PW loop and PT-21 to PT-28 on the WFI loop, and at the in-line instruments that serve them.

## Purified water, loop LP-01

| Attribute | Alert level | Action level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conductivity, in-line, at 25 degrees C | 1.0 uS/cm | 1.2 uS/cm |
| Total organic carbon | 380 ppb | 450 ppb |
| Total viable count, 1 mL pour plate on R2A agar | 40 CFU per mL | 80 CFU per mL |

## Water for injection, loop LP-02

| Attribute | Alert level | Action level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conductivity, in-line, at 25 degrees C | 0.8 uS/cm | 1.0 uS/cm |
| Total organic carbon | 300 ppb | 400 ppb |
| Total viable count, 100 mL by membrane filtration on R2A agar | 5 CFU per 100 mL | 8 CFU per 100 mL |
| Bacterial endotoxin | 0.10 EU per mL | 0.20 EU per mL |

Endotoxin is specified for WFI only. The frequency of endotoxin testing for PW follows the water system monitoring plan WMP-0011.

## Microbiological method

Plates are incubated at 20 to 25 degrees C for 5 days on R2A agar (MAT-5010). Samples for microbial testing are collected in sterile 100 mL bottles (MAT-5015), and the bottle is filled to the mark and no further. A membrane from a WFI sample that shows more than 50 colonies is recorded as a count of more than 50 and is treated as an action-level result whatever the exact number.

## Chemical method

In-line conductivity and TOC readings are taken from the instruments EQ-1101 and EQ-1102 on LP-01 and EQ-1201 and EQ-1202 on LP-02. Each in-line reading is checked against an offline measurement once a week, and the laboratory conductivity meter EQ-0845, checked with the 1.30 uS/cm standard MAT-2610, is used for the offline conductivity check. The offline and in-line conductivity readings must agree within 0.1 uS/cm, and the offline and in-line TOC readings within 50 ppb; a larger difference makes the in-line reading unusable until the instrument has been checked.

## Reporting of results

Conductivity is reported to 2 decimal places and TOC to the nearest 10 ppb. Microbial counts are reported as whole colonies, and the unit is always stated, since the PW result is per mL and the WFI result is per 100 mL. Results are entered on FRM-QC-201 and on the sampling record FRM-EN-301, and a result at or above its alert level is marked so that it can be seen without opening the record.

## Use of the levels

An alert level is a signal that the system is drifting, and it starts the response in SOP-EN-152 without any batch decision. An action level means the water from that point must not be used until the point has been resolved. The levels are reviewed every 3 years, or after a structural change to a loop, whichever comes first. A level is never relaxed on the strength of a single good month of data.
