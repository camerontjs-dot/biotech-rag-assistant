# Cleaning and Line Clearance control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls cleaning and line clearance at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to Operations personnel performing cleaning and line clearance work in R-cleaning-295 (Grade C). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 163 CFU per unit. Action limit means 165 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 110 hours. Clean hold time means 55 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-cleaning-193 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-cleaning-437 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-cleaning-302 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-cleaning-193 status in R-cleaning-295 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 10 samples using MAT-cleaning-437 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-cleaning-302.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 165 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 163 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance EQ-cleaning-193 R-cleaning-295 FRM-cleaning-302.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 55 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 110 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance EQ-cleaning-193 R-cleaning-295 FRM-cleaning-302.

5. Store samples at 8 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance R-cleaning-295 EQ-cleaning-193.

6. Complete review within 5 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance FRM-cleaning-302 R-cleaning-295.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance EQ-cleaning-193 R-cleaning-295.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Cleaning and Line Clearance R-cleaning-295.
