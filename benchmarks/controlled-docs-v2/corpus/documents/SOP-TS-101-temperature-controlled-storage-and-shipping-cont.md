# Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls temperature-controlled storage and shipping at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to Logistics personnel performing temperature-controlled storage and shipping work in R-temp-267 (Grade D). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 249 CFU per unit. Action limit means 251 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 158 hours. Clean hold time means 79 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-temp-131 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-temp-419 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-temp-380 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-temp-131 status in R-temp-267 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 3 samples using MAT-temp-419 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-temp-380.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 251 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 249 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping EQ-temp-131 R-temp-267 FRM-temp-380.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 79 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 158 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping EQ-temp-131 R-temp-267 FRM-temp-380.

5. Store samples at 3 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping R-temp-267 EQ-temp-131.

6. Complete review within 10 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping FRM-temp-380 R-temp-267.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping EQ-temp-131 R-temp-267.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Temperature-Controlled Storage and Shipping R-temp-267.
