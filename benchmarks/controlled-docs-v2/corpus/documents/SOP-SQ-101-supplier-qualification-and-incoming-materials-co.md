# Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials control procedure

## Purpose

This synthetic procedure controls supplier qualification and incoming materials at Synthetic Pilot Facility SPF-01 for Synthetic Test Compound STC-001. It defines responsibilities, limits, frequencies, and records. All values are fictional and carry no regulatory meaning.

## Scope

This procedure applies to QC personnel performing supplier qualification and incoming materials work in R-supplier-203 (Grade C). It does not apply to off-domain facility services.

## Definitions

Alert limit means 211 CFU per unit. Action limit means 213 CFU per unit. Dirty hold time means 134 hours. Clean hold time means 67 hours. Purified water (PW) is distinct from water for injection (WFI); this procedure uses PW unless WFI is explicitly stated.

## Materials and equipment

- Equipment EQ-supplier-190 calibrated per its schedule.
- Material MAT-supplier-414 sampled per the sampling plan.
- Form FRM-supplier-314 for batch and monitoring records.

## Procedure

1. Verify EQ-supplier-190 status in R-supplier-203 before starting. Do not start if calibration is expired.

2. Collect 5 samples using MAT-supplier-414 containers. Label each with room, date, and form FRM-supplier-314.

3. Incubate and read results against the action limit of 213 CFU per unit. The alert limit of 211 CFU per unit requires trending only; it is not a rejection limit. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials EQ-supplier-190 R-supplier-203 FRM-supplier-314.

4. Hold cleaned equipment for at most 67 hours (clean hold time). Dirty hold time must not exceed 134 hours. Record start and end times. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials EQ-supplier-190 R-supplier-203 FRM-supplier-314.

5. Store samples at 14 C plus or minus 2 C. Unless an excursion is confirmed, product remains available for further processing. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials R-supplier-203 EQ-supplier-190.

6. Complete review within 5 days. Only QA may close the record, and only if all acceptance criteria are met. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials FRM-supplier-314 R-supplier-203.

7. If viable monitoring exceeds the action limit, open a deviation before disposition. Except for pre-approved retest conditions, do not discard the original result. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials EQ-supplier-190 R-supplier-203.

8. Training is required before unsupervised work. Retraining is not required for editorial document changes that do not alter steps or limits. Area context: Supplier Qualification and Incoming Materials R-supplier-203.
