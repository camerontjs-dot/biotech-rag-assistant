# Change Control

## Purpose

Anything that could alter the validated state of a process, a system, a room, a material or a document has to be assessed before it is made and confirmed afterwards. This procedure sets out how a change is proposed, classified, assessed, approved, carried out and reviewed, and how urgent and temporary changes are handled. It applies to manufacturing, laboratory, engineering, warehouse and quality systems at the site.

## What needs a change request

A change request is needed for any alteration to equipment, software, facilities, utilities, materials, suppliers, methods, specifications, labels or process parameters that is not a like-for-like maintenance action inside an approved instruction. The classification matrix in SPC-2245 lists typical changes and their classes. A purely editorial correction to a document, such as a typing error, is made under SOP-QA-105 and does not need a change request.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Change owner | Raises the request, coordinates the assessment, carries out the change |
| Functional reviewers | Assess impact in their own area and record it on FRM-QA-304 |
| QA reviewer | Checks the request, assigns the class, owns the record |
| Change board | Approves or rejects Class 2 and Class 3 changes |
| QA Head | Gives verbal approval of emergency changes |

## Raising the request

The change owner completes FRM-QA-301 with the reason for the change, the current and proposed states, the affected areas and any dates that constrain the work. The request receives a number in the form CN-nnnn within 1 day of being logged. The QA reviewer checks the request and assigns its class within 2 working days of receiving it. Where the request is incomplete, it is returned to the owner with the missing items listed and the clock restarts on resubmission. An owner has 10 days to answer a query from a reviewer, and a request with no answer after 30 days is put on hold. The QA reviewer answers a query from an owner within 4 hours in normal working time, reminders go out 5 days before an assessment is due, and a change planned for a shutdown is submitted at least 8 weeks ahead.

## Impact assessment

Each functional reviewer named by the QA reviewer completes their part of FRM-QA-304 within 8 working days. The assessment covers validation status, documents to be revised, training, effects on other equipment or products, regulatory or customer notification, supply and cost. A reviewer who sees no effect states that and gives the reason; a blank entry is never accepted. The QA reviewer collates the assessments and prepares the package for the board.

## The change board

The board meets weekly on Wednesday, and it needs at least 4 members present, including one from QA and one from the area making the change, to take a decision. The package is circulated 2 days before the meeting. The board approves, rejects or returns each Class 2 and Class 3 request, and the decision, any conditions and the approvers are recorded. Class 1 changes are approved by the QA reviewer alone and are reported to the board for information in a summary every 30 days. The board minutes are issued within 2 days, and a rejected change may be resubmitted after 14 days with new information.

## Timelines by class

| Class | Implementation deadline from approval | Post-implementation review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 1 | 30 days | None |
| Class 2 | 60 days | 14 days after go-live |
| Class 3 | 75 days | 45 days after go-live |

A change that has not been implemented by its deadline is returned to the board, which either extends the date once or cancels the request. Implementation must not start before the board has approved the change, except for an emergency change.

## Emergency changes

An emergency change is one that cannot wait for the board because it protects product, people or a legal obligation. The QA Head can approve it verbally, and the approval, the time and the reason are noted at once by the QA reviewer. The paperwork, including FRM-QA-301 and the assessments, is completed within 2 working days. The board reviews the change at its next meeting, within 7 days, and may require it to be reversed.

## Temporary changes

A temporary change is one made for a fixed period and then undone, such as a substitute part or a modified hold. It may run for at most 45 days, with one extension of 30 days approved by the board. When the period ends the change expires automatically, and the owner either restores the original state or converts the temporary change into a permanent request. A temporary change is reviewed by its owner every 14 days, and one that is left running past its date is reported as a deviation under SOP-QA-101.

## Carrying out the change

The owner completes the implementation checklist FRM-QA-307 as work proceeds. It lists the revised documents and their effective dates, the training records, the equipment qualification or requalification needed, the verification batches in SPC-2245 and the notifications to customers or authorities. All affected staff, without exception, are trained before go-live: the target is 100 percent, and the QA reviewer will not close the checklist with a name missing. For a change that stays open longer than 30 days, the QA reviewer reviews the checklist every 7 days.

## Notification

Within 5 working days of approval, the QA reviewer assesses whether the change must be notified to customers or to an outside authority. The timeline for filing a post-approval change follows the regulatory affairs procedure RAP-0008, and Regulatory Affairs is consulted before any Class 3 change is implemented. The QA reviewer sets a reminder 7 days before the planned go-live so that any notification is sent in time.

## Verification and validation

Verification batches after implementation are set by class in SPC-2245. Where the change affects a validated process, the change owner states in the assessment what revalidation is needed. The number of comparability batches for a change that introduces a new product is taken from the validation master plan VMP-0002 for the product concerned.

## Post-implementation review

The review on FRM-QA-310 checks that the change did what was intended and nothing more. The owner compares the results of the verification batches with the expectations in the request, confirms that the documents and training are complete, and records any unexpected effects. A review that finds a problem is followed by a deviation, a CAPA or a further change, as appropriate.

## Cancellation

A request that has seen no activity for 180 days is cancelled by the QA reviewer after a warning to the owner. A cancelled request can be reopened only by raising a new one. Closed requests are archived 30 days after their post-implementation review.

## Links to other procedures

Where a change is the result of a CAPA action, it refers to the CAPA number, and SOP-QA-172 tracks the action. Changes of supplier are assessed with SOP-WH-064, and changes of cleaning agent or method are assessed with SOP-MF-207. Deviations raised under SOP-QA-101 that expose a need for a permanent change are converted into a request here.
