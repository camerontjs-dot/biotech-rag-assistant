# Environmental monitoring excursion response

## Purpose

This synthetic SOP describes how QA triages a viable environmental monitoring excursion in a controlled manufacturing suite.

## Immediate containment

When viable monitoring exceeds an action limit, QA opens a deviation before product disposition. The area owner places affected room status, related product lots, and exposed materials on hold until QA documents the initial impact assessment.

## Investigation record

The investigation record must identify the sample location, sampling date, organism result if available, affected room, affected product lots, containment action, and the person who approved reopening the room.
