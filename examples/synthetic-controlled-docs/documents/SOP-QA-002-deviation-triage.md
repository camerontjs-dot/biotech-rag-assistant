# Deviation triage and escalation

## Purpose

This synthetic SOP defines how quality events are triaged before a full investigation starts.

## Triage categories

Minor deviations may be contained within the owning department when there is no product impact. Major deviations require QA review, documented impact assessment, and escalation to the quality council when repeat events suggest a process weakness.

## Closure rule

A deviation cannot close until the record identifies root cause status, immediate containment, corrective action if needed, and the owner for any preventive action.
