Proposed experiment identity: `qualifier-grounding-offline-qualification-v2`.
Execution: **NOT_RUN**. Candidate: **NOT_IMPLEMENTED_OR_FROZEN**.

The v1 candidate and its single decisive run terminate FALSIFIED. A material
candidate repair must have a new commit/tree, freeze, reveal record, and experiment
identity. It cannot be counted as another pass of v1.

The smallest preparation step is to specify the normative meaning of retained
core_ids for complete claims, citation enums, empty fields on rejected decisions,
and global uncertainty from conflicting assessments or unknown safety. Preserve
the v1 policy/interface/controls/expectations unchanged; any clarification that
changes them needs its own new frozen package identity.

The owner of v1 has now seen every fixed control and expectation. Reusing these
controls after repair is regression evidence. A successor qualification must
separately preregister fresh or independently accepted discriminating controls,
including retention and uncertainty cases, and bind all artifacts before reveal.
Do not describe adaptation to v1 answers as fresh independent qualification.

The successor keeps the same narrow question, assessment_ledger → decision_record,
with zero model/provider calls and no generation, gate replay, protected payload
access, CAL, ADR-018 change, promotion, merge, or release. Wave B remains LOCKED.

An unresolved later semantic-assessor question is whether an assessor can account
for every material proposition and its bindings in exact natural-language claims
and authorized BODY evidence without importing query/heading meaning. That is a
new semantic experiment and is not implemented or executed here.
