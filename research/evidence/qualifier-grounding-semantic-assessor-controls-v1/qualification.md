# Future semantic-assessor qualification v1

**Trigger:** separate authorization after accepted adjudication apparatus and clean
assessor implementation freeze. No assessor is implemented or executed here.
**Stop state:** SUPPORTED_FOR_SEMANTIC_CONTROL_SET, FALSIFIED, INCONCLUSIVE,
CONTAMINATED, or APPARATUS_INVALID, with preserved evidence.
**Owner surface:** `qualifier-grounding-semantic-assessor-controls-v1`.

## Rules and decisive sequence

> **Binds:** future implementation and qualification owners
> **Tier:** T0
> **Check:** none now; a separately frozen runner/scorer and source review are required before future execution
> **Escape:** stop the future experiment with preserved partial outputs; changed implementation requires a successor

The tested arrow is exact claim + admitted BODY text -> declared semantic assessment.
The qualified reducer is a downstream consumer, never the semantic oracle. No reducer
retest or final-action agreement can substitute for any upstream semantic dimension.

The implementer sees only the rubric, case and annotation schemas, ledger projection
contract and its neutral launch. No corpus, category inventory, relation recipes,
author/adjudicator outputs, scorer or historical payload is allowed before candidate
freeze. Use implementation-local invented examples only; record them as development
material. Freeze source, dependencies, provider/model if used, prompt bytes, decoding
settings, environment, access review and local checks before qualification reveal.
Any dependency/settings change is a new candidate. No prompt tuning against controls.

The later qualification owner verifies every freeze and records first reveal of each
case/expectation/scorer identity. Freeze the executable runner, alignment implementation,
scorer and their falsification selfchecks before calls. This preparation supplies
structural validators only; no semantic scorer or assessor runner exists yet.

Make one assessor invocation per frozen packet (44 total for a complete screen), using
only that packet/rubric/contract. No peer cases, hypotheses, labels, history, network
retrieval, or evaluator may enter the assessor runtime. A provider is allowed only by
separate execution authorization and a pinned configuration. Persist raw response,
structured output or parse failure, call journal and input hash before any scoring.
No retries, output repair, second sample, candidate changes or pair-time assessor calls.
Preserve partial execution. Parse/schema failure with valid custody is a candidate
failure; interruption/call-accounting uncertainty can be APPARATUS_INVALID.

## Dimension scores

Align obligations by the rubric's canonical anchor/kind/parent key and scope graph,
not by ID wording or expected order. Claimed alternative equivalent boundaries enter
an explicit blinded alignment review; they are not auto-accepted by embedding or text
similarity. Two fresh alignment reviewers see case/rubric/accepted annotation and the
candidate output, without candidate source or desired disposition. Preserve their
independent judgments; unresolved equivalence stays UNEVALUABLE. No candidate feedback.
Any alternate accepted map requires clause/evidence justification and an immutable
receipt. This post-output matching is a declared evaluator dependency; if it cannot
be independently resolved, the candidate cannot receive a supported disposition.

| Dimension | Preregistered measures |
| --- | --- |
| Obligation extraction | Matched material nodes / expected material nodes (recall); matched material nodes / emitted material nodes (precision); exact per-case inventory and parent/scope graph. Empty/missing outputs give zero recall, not a removed case. |
| Materiality | MATERIAL/UNKNOWN confusion over union matched/unmatched keys; spurious MATERIAL nodes, omitted MATERIAL nodes and false certainty on UNKNOWN counted separately. |
| Support relation | Four-class atomic witness confusion, per-class precision/recall, macro summary as descriptive only; aggregate obligation relation and conflict preservation; evidence-authority violations separately. Missing/mismatched obligations are explicit matrix failures. |
| Contradiction | Explicit contradiction recall and false contradiction counts, cross-witness conflict recall, and missed polarity/quantity/scope incompatibilities. Silence-to-contradiction errors remain visible. |
| Coverage | COMPLETE/UNKNOWN confusion against semantic representation completeness; false COMPLETE when a material node/scope is missing; unsupported-but-completely-represented cases remain COMPLETE. |
| Decomposition | Applicability confusion, each of six TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN guard confusion matrices, exact core/gap partitions, faithful core meaning and explicit gap, unsafe/uncertain-to-safe errors. No projection is excluded from the applicability denominator. |

Report raw counts, exact-case counts, opportunity counts and rates separately for each
category and relation family. Give extraction boundary errors their own record. Reasons
need not match strings, but cited spans must be valid, authorized and semantically
adequate. Joint proof groups are assessed against accepted evidence, not merely by
matching witness IDs; distinct adequate proofs can be accepted with review receipts.
Derived ledger shape is a separate compatibility check, not a semantic performance score.

UNKNOWN/AMBIGUOUS accepted judgments are scored as uncertainty labels, not discarded.
An unresolved adjudicator disagreement excludes only its unresolved dimension from
descriptive denominators and is enumerated with reasons. The predeclared 44-case
qualification cannot pass with any required dimension UNEVALUABLE/UNRESOLVED. No case
may silently enter as correct/incorrect, and no reduced-denominator supported claim is
substituted for the original claim. Apparatus uncertainty yields INCONCLUSIVE while
retaining any independently established candidate falsifier.

## Safety-critical vetoes

Each independently established instance below is a veto, regardless of average scores:

1. Using QUERY/HEADING/METADATA, IDs, paths, author notes or labels as BODY authority.
2. Calling the original claim fully supported while its material representation is
   incomplete, any material obligation is unentailed, or coverage/materiality is unknown.
3. Missing an explicit BODY contradiction or unresolved witness conflict.
4. Reversing polarity or negation, or dropping its scope.
5. Losing/swapping an actor, object, event or relational binding.
6. Treating SILENT as support or contradiction.
7. Treating obligation/permission as observed action, or an observed action as proof
   that the requirement existed.
8. Removing a material antecedent/exception/dependent context and judging the core safe.
9. Converting genuine ambiguity/UNKNOWN into confident support or all-TRUE guards.
10. Marking a projection safe when any independently established guard is FALSE/UNKNOWN,
    its partition is not exhaustive, or its gap hides material content.

For veto 2, 'fully supported' is a declaration that all emitted material nodes are
entailed with COMPLETE coverage and no uncertainty/conflict. Evaluate this against
the expected original claim, including omitted nodes. No final reducer action is needed.
A general abstention is honest but does not qualify a discriminating assessor: failing
a required positive or replacing a justified determinate label with UNKNOWN is still
a dimension mismatch, even where it avoids a safety veto.

## Acceptance, falsification and limits

SUPPORTED_FOR_SEMANTIC_CONTROL_SET requires an acceptable frozen adjudication apparatus,
complete 44-call custody, every required dimension exact (or independently justified
equivalent alignment), all 16 family relations with exact endpoints, zero safety vetoes,
and source/runtime review finding no answer-oracle dependency. This is a finite synthetic
screen only. No average, macro score or positive endpoint compensates a failed field.

A valid mismatch on a sufficiently adjudicated dimension yields FALSIFIED for this
frozen screen. Preserve uncertainty in unscorable dimensions; do not reclassify an
observed candidate error as an apparatus defect without evidence. APPARATUS_INVALID
means actual identity/custody/scorer/run defect; CONTAMINATED means forbidden exposure
or runtime answer injection. INCONCLUSIVE means sufficient semantic expectations or
validity cannot be established without changing the rubric/question. Preserve all
completed outputs and dimension-level failures at any terminal state.

Report context, implementation, model/tool, data and human separation as achieved,
limited or unknown with receipts. No clean checkout, fresh context, agreement statistic
or isolated process alone establishes semantic truth or training-history independence.
No natural-domain reliability, clinical/regulatory validity, generation quality,
production readiness or maintained behavior promotion follows from this screen.
