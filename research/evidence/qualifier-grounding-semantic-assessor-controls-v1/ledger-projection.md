# Semantic annotation to frozen v2 ledger projection

**Trigger:** separately authorized future adapter construction after semantic annotation.
**Stop state:** a structurally valid ledger or explicit failure/uncertainty; no reducer
execution is authorized by this preparation.
**Owner surface:** `qualifier-grounding-semantic-assessor-controls-v1`.

## Projection rules

> **Binds:** future adapter implementer and qualification reviewer
> **Tier:** T0
> **Check:** none now; the frozen v2 interface defines later structural compatibility
> **Escape:** stop on an unrepresentable assessment; never change v2 semantics to hide it

Use `case_id` and the exact emitted obligation IDs. Every assertion/facet retained in
the annotation becomes one ledger obligation with its MATERIAL/UNKNOWN declaration.
Map coverage verbatim. Validated packet/source custody and successful annotation allow
integrity VALID; missing custody or failed annotation is an apparatus integrity failure.
The adapter cannot certify semantic correctness or override UNKNOWN materiality.

For each single-span BODY witness use surface BODY and apertures [P]. A relation row
adds its obligation ID to entails, contradicts or ambiguous according to its label;
SILENT adds no assertion. No BODY span is a Q quote or N nomination in this first-arrow
screen. QUERY/HEADING/METADATA may be represented with their own surface and empty
apertures and empty semantic sets, or omitted. They never contribute a BODY relation.

For a justified joint proof, create a composite BODY/P witness with a generated opaque
ID, preserving in the annotation/projection receipt every constituent admitted span,
exact evidence anchor and identity-link inference. It is a declaration over admitted
BODY, not a new source. Preserve all constituent witnesses, especially contradictions
or ambiguity. No composite can erase those rows or resolve cross-witness conflict.
Cross-span E/C overlap remains overlap; an internally ambiguous group enters ambiguous.

Map an applicable projection's actual retained/gap obligation IDs and contributing
BODY/composite witnesses, with TRUE -> true, FALSE -> false, UNKNOWN -> null for each
of the six guards. Do not fabricate a safe partition when supplied wording is unsafe.
A NOT_REQUIRED projection maps to null. Unresolved applicability maps to null, with
its uncertainty already represented by UNKNOWN coverage/materiality or an ambiguous
authorized assessment; if none can faithfully carry the uncertainty, stop and record
an adapter representability defect. Do not encode semantic doubt as integrity VALID
with a fabricated determinate proposal.

The adapter performs no inference from source prose and imports no case/expected
answer lookup. All semantic judgments precede it. Reducer/citation outputs remain
outside adjudicator inputs and semantic truth. Absence of Q/N intentionally limits
citation/emission authority; it does not remove P support. The reducer's supported
symbolic contract is preserved exactly, without retest in this preparation.
